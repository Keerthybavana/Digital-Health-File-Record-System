About HealthDoc ML

HealthDoc ML is a secure, cloud-enabled health file cabinet. We utilize advanced machine learning classification to categorize clinical PDFs automatically, giving patients and doctors instant, organized access to records.

Technology Stack
  • Flask & SQLite Database
  • Scikit-Learn (Logistic Regression)
  • PyPDF2 Text Extraction
  • Dynamically Rendered QR Codes
